About Me

Mathematician turned data professional in Ghana's pensions industry.

I'm a Senior Business Analyst at SSNIT, where I've spent over seven years in analytics after seven years in data management — giving me a rare end-to-end view of how social-security data is captured, validated, and turned into decisions.

My work spans data engineering (SQL Server, Oracle, SSIS, Tableau Prep), data science (Python, scikit-learn, predictive models for benefit payments and contribution collection), and business intelligence (Tableau Server administration and KPI dashboards used across departments).

With a foundation in Mathematics and Economics, I enjoy deriving the formulas behind the numbers — like the closed-form penalty computation and vectorised benefit-accrual methods now used in the Trust's operations. I also train and supervise analytics staff, and contributed to setting SSNIT's medium-term strategic objectives.

Featured Projects

Featured · BI Dashboard

"Big Push" Projects Compliance Dashboard

An interactive Tableau dashboard monitoring SSNIT compliance across Ghana's Big Push infrastructure projects — tracking 74 projects, contractor registrations, labour-force dynamics, inspection recency, and establishment liability status, filterable by area, enforcement branch, and contractor. Used by enforcement teams to prioritise site inspections and recover contributions for construction workers.

TableauSQLETLCompliance Analytics

Closed-Form Penalty Computation for Defaulted Contributions

Derived a formula computing the total penalty payable on defaulted pension contributions by combining the sum of a Geometric Progression with Compound Interest — replacing tedious month-by-month calculation with a single closed-form expression, including handling of part-payments made in between. Authored the methodology paper and implemented it in Excel for operational use.

MathematicsExcelPolicy Modelling

Benefit Accrual Estimation for Accrual Accounting

Designed the methodology estimating SSNIT's benefit liabilities under accrual accounting — covering processed-but-unpaid claims and eligible members yet to claim. Derived a vectorised lumpsum/past-credit accumulation formula whose pre-computable factor vectors make large-scale member computations efficient, with support for PNDCL 247 and Act 766 benefit schemes.
